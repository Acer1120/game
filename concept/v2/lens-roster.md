## 0. What the data actually says

- **87 CFG kinds.** 48 are hand-written in `CFG` (including the hidden `blob` and the summon-only `mirrorimage`), and 39 are generated by `NEWKINDS`. There are **64 sprite bodies** (every `HVU_META` key has an `Idle` strip). The other **23 kinds borrow a body** and change its tint and scale: captain on `watch`, berserker on `orc`, warlock on `necro`, warden on `templar`, headhunter on `archer`, warchief on `eliteorc`, revenant on `ironskel`, hierophant on `priest`, mesmer on `wizard`, plaguebearer on `slimekingpink`, and all 13 capstone blocks. `SLIMELET` is a module constant, not a CFG kind. The rest of the 478 sheets are effect layers (`*Fx`), shadow strips (`watchShadow*`, `orcShadow*`) and projectiles (`arrow*`, `lancerBolt*`, `beamFx`, `eyebeamFx`, `ghostbeamFx`, `spikeFx`, `darkboltFx`, `arrowDemon`, `cannonball`, `spit{Green,Blue,Pink,Orange,Dark}`).
- **EVOLVE splits into 21 connected lineages plus 3 loners** (`minotaur`, `blob`, `mirrorimage`). Evolution edges ignore `GROUPS` 15 times: axeman→ironorc, lancer→pike→rider, knife→verdagger, archer→demonarcher, hellbat→bloodwing, hellhound→werewolf, lavaslime→flamegolem, toxcrawler→plaguebearer, harpy→siren, and every edge into the `custom` capstones. **Those crossings are the world's migrations, and the story should be built on them** (§3).
- **17 terminal capstones sit behind a tier-5 edge:** warden, timewarden, mirrorknight, spellblade, warchief, gravelord, revenant, lich, plaguedoctor, magmacolossus, abysslord, stormwing, siren, demonbrute, werebear, voidreaper, inquisitor. Two more kinds are tier-5 *steps* rather than endpoints: `flamegolem` (lavaslime@5, then magmacolossus@5) and `noctislicer` (duskclaw@5, then voidreaper@5). `evolve()` only checks `e.tier >= opt.tier`, so a tier-5 unit can chain lavaslime → flamegolem → magmacolossus in one sitting.
- **`boss:true` splits the capstones in two.** Seven are boss-flagged: warchief, magmacolossus, abysslord, stormwing, voidreaper, timewarden, gravelord. Ten are not: warden, mirrorknight, spellblade, revenant, lich, plaguedoctor, siren, demonbrute, werebear, inquisitor. Six more boss-flagged kinds are not capstones: the roots `demonlord` (unlock 11) and `frostfiend` (11), the loner `minotaur` (10), and the three tier-4 slime kings.
- **Twelve lines end at tier 4 with no tier-5 capstone:** headhunter, demonarcher, mesmer, hierophant, rider, bonearcher2, blackknight, ghostfire, pumpkinred, succubus, plaguebearer, and the three slime kings.
- **`evolve()` works on any body at tier 5, enemies included.** Enemies spawn at tier 5 by default (line 289). `evolve` keeps team and hunt and plays `rite(n,'EVOLVED')`. The world can therefore evolve a lord *in front of the player*, with the same rite the player's own unit will later play.
- **The capstones already wear a crown in the demo.** Every `group==='custom'` unit gets a dashed accent ring and a crown glyph (units.js ~2117). `drawInfo().custom` is true for `group==='custom'` *or* `isAdvanced(e)` (UNIQUE at level 40, or ASCENDED). The render contract already treats "a lord" and "a player unit that has become legendary" as the same kind of thing.
- **"Hallokin" appears only three times, all in the guide comments** (lines 33, 51, 55), and nowhere on screen. The game's own name for the hero is "the watchman" (`src/10-config.js` line 3).
- **Name hazards:** `pumpkinred` is displayed as **THE HOLLOW KING**, with the passive **HOLLOW**. `timewarden` is displayed as **THE CHRONO TEMPLAR**. `warden` exists both as `HVU.CFG.warden` (THE WARDEN) and as the game's slime boss key in `19b-archetypes.js` (line 44, `warden:'THE WARDEN'`).

### FAMILY_MINIONS against the lineages
`pathRise` (the CONJURER path, line 326) summons `FAMILY_MINIONS[c.group]`. Two entries fight the lineages. **`custom` → ironskel + boneblade** means a conjuring Inquisitor, Plague Doctor or Storm Wing raises bones. **`men` → watch + skel** means a Templar raises a skeleton. The fix is small and pays off in the fiction: key the minion table by lineage root instead of by group. A conjurer then raises the **tier-1 roots of its own line**: an Inquisitor raises cannoneers, a Plague Doctor raises venom slimes (its region's blot), a Storm Wing raises bats, a Lich keeps bones. The rule becomes "a finished figure calls up first sketches of itself", which the player can read without a tooltip.

## 2. The factions, redrawn around lineages

**Rule: a faction is a set of lineages, not a set of accent colours.** A lineage belongs to the faction that holds its *root and its capstone*. Edges that cross between factions are defections (§3). v1's accent-based reading still works at a glance, because the lineage accents mostly agree: Watch steel-grey, Order gold, bone, Redrafter violet, Warband green, and now Kindled red.

### 2.2 The Kindled: where the demons go

**What they are.** Lamp-flame is ink turned into light, and a lamp burns only Well-ink. When the Well cracked, the southern Great Lamp at **Cinder Gate** ran dry. Its Watch kept it lit by feeding it **colour scraped off the land around it**. The flame took the colour and came alive. The Kindled are *colour that was taken instead of kept*: the world's colour-means-care rule turned inside out.

Their data reads as hunger. Most of the family drains:
- **LEECH** on bloodfiend, bloodwing, succubus and shade.
- **drainOnHit** on the Abyss Lord.
- **SWARM** on imp, gremlin and hellbat.

They charm, which is care taken by force: KISS (succubus) and CHORUS / SONG (siren). And they burn: burn, `molten`, `fireAura`, and the pillar strikes HELLFIRE, INFERNO and BLACK FLAME. Every one of those rules can be shown on screen without a codex.

**Who is who, by lineage:**
- **THE DEMON LORD** (`demonlord`, boss-flagged root at unlock 11) is the Cinder Gate flame itself, the first thing that walked out of the lamp. DREAD fits: near it, you fight with less.
- **THE ABYSS LORD** (`abysslord`, blue-violet `#2a2a8a`, tint [0.55,0.55,1.0]) is that flame burned hot enough to go blue and through the paper. Behind a burn hole is the back of the page, so OBLIVION ("pulls everything in") is a body falling into the hole the Abyss Lord burned. This ties the Kindled to v1's back-of-the-page secrets.
- **The Choir** (gremlin → imp → succubus / demon brute; bloodfiend and harpy → succubus; harpy → siren) are the lamp's sparks, grown. Small ones swarm (gremlin, imp). Grown ones either tempt (succubus, siren) or just eat (demon brute, BLOODBATH).
- **The Forge** (lavaslime → flamegolem → magmacolossus). A lava slime is a blot of Well-ink that caught fire. Its GROUPS entry is `beasts` and its lineage is Kindled: it was Wild ink until it burned. The Magma Colossus is the Kiln where the Kindled make more fire.
- **The Cinder Watch** (beamknight → blackknight). These are the Watchmen of Cinder Gate who fed the lamp. They wear armour in Watch-adjacent steel (`#5a6a8a`, `#4a4a58`) and fight with fire pillars (INFERNO, BLACK FLAME). **They are the Watch's secret shame, and the reason the Watch and the Kindled cannot make peace.**
- **Hellhounds and blood wings** are the Kindled's hounds and air. Both lineages *leave* the Kindled (§3).
- **The demon archer** is a hired bow (archer → demonarcher). Its KEEN passive fits a mercenary.
- **THE MINOTAUR** is *not* Kindled, even though v30 GROUPS lists it under `demons`. It has no lineage, and its accent equals the werebear's (`#6a4a3a`), so it belongs to the Hatch (§2.3).
- **THE EYEBALL / GHOSTFIRE** are also in GROUPS `demons` but are *not* Kindled. They are the Lampless (§2.3).

**The three false cures.** The Order would *still* the page (Varnish). The Redrafters would *trace over* it (Trace). The Kindled would *burn* it (Burn). Each would stop the Hollow and end everything alive. The Watch's answer is the only slow one: tend the lamps. This gives the middle of the story three antagonists with one shape, and it gives the demons a reason to exist that only this world could supply.

### 2.3 Not factions: the Wild, the Dusk and the Hollow

- **THE WILD** is hostile to all and political to none. It has three kinds of body, each tied to a physical force from v1 §3.1:
  - **The Blot** (loose ink): slime, slimeblue, slimepink, slimekinggreen, slimeking, slimekingpink, toxcrawler → plaguebearer, blob, SLIMELET. The three kings are the only boss-flagged tops of their lines, so the Spill's lords are its kings.
  - **The Hatch** (beasts in true shadow): bat → hellbat (L14's Wild end), werewolf, werebear (L15), minotaur. The Hatch's crown is **THE STORM WING**, the one capstone that no house can claim.
  - **The Lampless** (new): eyeball → ghostfire, pumpkin → pumpkinred. **A snuffed lamp does not simply go out: its watching wanders off as an EYEBALL (GAZE marks whatever it sees), its flame as a GHOSTFIRE (FLICKER), and its lantern as a PUMPKINHEAD** (a carved gourd with a flame inside, passive HOLLOW, "absorbs a hit"). Relighting a lamp calls them home: they walk into the flame and scrub out. This replaces v1's "a werewolf turns back into a citizen", which v30's lineages no longer support (werewolves now come from hellhounds).
- **THE DUSK** (L16, the whole shadow line) is the hatch that grows *without* a beast inside it. Nightfang, verdagger, duskclaw, noctislicer and shade live in the dark between lamps, and **the Hollow herds them**. Shadow is the last drawn thing before blank, so they walk ahead of the erasure like crows ahead of a storm. Two roots are telling. **THE FROST FIEND**'s ULT is literally called WHITEOUT: a blizzard is the Hollow's weather. **THE VOID REAPER** is the Dusk's crown and the lord of the Margin. Its EVENT HORIZON pulls the drawn world toward the torn edge. The Dusk is *not* the Hollow: it has colour (deep violets and greens), it boils, and it can be fought and bound. It is the Hollow's army because it is the only thing that thrives where the Hollow has been.
- **THE HOLLOW** stays an absence with no units. The **HOLLOWED** affix (v1 §4.4) stands unchanged and now covers 87 kinds. A HOLLOWED capstone ("HOLLOWED GRAVELORD") is the Margin's elite.

## 4. The capstone's double role: the lord you fought is the unit you become

### 4.1 The fiction: one finished figure per line

An artist draws a figure again and again, each study stronger than the last, until one drawing is *finished*. **Every lineage is a study sequence, and its tier-5 capstone is the finished figure the Draftsman meant.** He drew one finished figure of each line and put the pen down. The lords are those finished figures, and they took the land their drawing was made for.

This is why a lord rules. In a world that stays real only while it is watched, the most finished drawing on a sheet is the one the sheet believes in. But the lords *keep their drawing and let the lamps go out*. They hoard colour (the Kindled burn it, the Order varnishes it, the Gravelord keeps the dead) instead of caring for it.

**You cannot take a lord's drawing. You can only draw it again yourself.** When your unit evolves into THE GRAVELORD, you have not captured the lord. You have made a second finished figure of the same line, and this one keeps the lamps. The sheet now has two drawings of its lord and has to choose which to believe. That choice is the crown.

ASCENDED finishes the idea. `ascendAt(e)` is 30% above `fullBuildPower(kind)`, the power of that kind fully built, and renown from kills closes the gap. An ASCENDED capstone is *drawn better than the Draftsman drew it*. The demo already gives ASCENDED its own accent (`#f0e6c0`) and UNIQUE its own (`#d4a017`), so the render layer is ready for this.

### 4.2 The rules in play

1. **Capstones are grown, never bound.** The 17 terminal tier-5 kinds (§0) cannot be SPARED onto your side, whether lord, champion or Hollowed. The only way to own one is to raise a unit of its line to tier 5 (level 30, `readyTier` + `advance`) and `evolve` it. This keeps "I became the lord" precious, and it stops a spare from skipping 30 levels.
2. **Everything else can be bound, and joins at tier 1.** A spared body is redrawn as your first sketch of it: `spawnFrom({kind, tier:1, level:1})`, with the T1 kit (first move only, plain AI, line 283). That includes the boss-flagged roots (`demonlord`, `frostfiend`), the loner `minotaur` and the slime kings. **Binding the Demon Lord is the only road to your own Abyss Lord.** The Cinder Gate's tyrant kneels as a tier-1 flame that has to be kept for 30 levels.
3. **You cannot draw what you have never seen finished.** A capstone EVOLVE edge stays greyed out in the evolution preview (a silhouette with "a figure you have not seen finished") until that kind's Roster page is **BESTED**: you have beaten its lord or champion once. Lower edges open on SEEN. This is where v1's Roster (seen / studied / bested / bound) meets v30's graph: *knowledge gates evolution, and XP gates tier.*
4. **Lord fights end with a live evolution.** Every boss-flagged lord is met in its tier-4 (or root) body and evolves at a phase threshold through `HVU.evolve(e, into)`. The rite plays (`fx.rite`, the `EVOLVED` mark) and the capstone steps out at full health: a second phase the module already knows how to do. The player sees the exact rite their own unit will play later, on the body they will later earn.
   - greatskel → **gravelord** (the Palimpsest)
   - templar → **timewarden** (Vellum Abbey)
   - eliteorc → **warchief** (the Brushlands)
   - demonlord → **abysslord** (Cinder Gate)
   - flamegolem → **magmacolossus** (the Kiln)
   - bloodwing → **stormwing** (the Hatchwood)
   - noctislicer → **voidreaper** (the Margin)
5. **Beaten, the lord offers two endings.** (v1 said "lords are never spared". v30's army changes that, but they still never join you.)
   - **TAKE THE CROWN (execute).** You gain its Law (v1 §6.3), and the crown is vacant.
   - **LET IT KEEP THE LAMP (spare).** The lord withdraws to its Great Lamp and *tends it for you*: the region's Defend Vigils stop, and its house gains +2 standing. You get no Law, and the crown stays with the lord.
   - Either choice opens the capstone edge.
6. **The crown.** If a lord's crown is vacant, the **first** of your units to evolve into that kind is crowned. It gets a `label` ("THE GRAVELORD OF THE PALIMPSEST": `serialize` already carries `label` and the demo already draws it) and the crown glyph that group-`custom` bodies already wear. You may own many Gravelords, but only one is crowned.
7. **Post it, or march with it.** A crowned unit can be **posted as WARDEN of its region's Great Lamp** (§6). While posted, that sheet is tended: no Defend Vigils, and lamps inside it do not gutter. But the unit is not in your field party. **Your best units either hold the land or fight beside you, never both.** That is the army-management decision v30's roster was built for, and it gives the "territory" leg of v1's progression a body.
8. **Champions** are the ten capstones without `boss:true`. They are fought as **duels** with the Signed's grammar: letterbox slit, `bossNameEl`, flee at the ULT threshold, remember. They carry no Law. Beating one BESTs its page and opens its edge. They still cannot be bound.

### 4.3 What this does to the whole progression

A unit's life is now the world's story told in miniature:

> bind a first sketch → raise it (XP from its own kills) → take a path at tier 2 → at each fork, choose which house it joins (§3) → beat the finished figure → evolve it → take the crown → post it on a lamp, or ascend it

Each step uses a v30 system that already exists (`grantXp`, PATHS, EVOLVE, `advance`, `label`, `checkAscension`), and each step is also a move in the world.

## 6. The Warden, revisited

### 6.1 What changed
v1 renamed the units module's shield-bearer to THE SENTINEL, moved it into the Gilt Order, and kept THE WARDEN for the slime boss. v30 invalidates half of that: `captain →@5 warden`, GROUPS puts `warden` in `watch`, and its accent `#7fa0d0` is Watch steel-blue, not Order gold. **The Warden unit is the top of the Watch's rank ladder, not an Order knight.**

### 6.2 Decision
- **The kind keeps its key, `warden`**, so saves and `EVOLVE` stay untouched. **Its CFG `name` becomes THE SENTINEL**, a one-string change as in v1, and its passive (BULWARK) and ULT (AEGIS) names stay. The Watch's ladder reads **THE WATCH → THE CAPTAIN → THE SENTINEL**, and the knight branch reads THE KNIGHT → THE MIRROR KNIGHT (or the gilt road to THE TEMPLAR → THE CHRONO TEMPLAR).
- **"Warden" is a post, not a kind.** A Warden is whoever the Watch sets to keep a Great Lamp. That is exactly v1's "Warden of the Well" and exactly §4.2 rule 7: a crowned unit posted on a Great Lamp is **WARDEN OF (sheet)**, shown through `label`. Any crowned capstone can hold the post (a posted Gravelord is WARDEN OF THE PALIMPSEST), but the Sentinel is the one built for it.
- **THE WARDEN the boss keeps its name and every shipped string** (the wave card, WARDEN SLAYER, the phase shouts). The fiction corrects one word from v1: *the Watch*, not the Order, posted its greatest Sentinel as Warden of the Well. The Well cracked, the Sentinel went in after the ink, and the ink kept the post. The fourth beat's **drowned Sentinel** is a real `warden` kind (THE SENTINEL). Sealing it raises **Watch** standing, not Order standing.
- **Your first crown.** Beating the Well's Warden vacates Ashridge's post. The first unit you raise from a `watch` through `captain` to `warden` becomes **WARDEN OF ASHRIDGE**. The first capstone a player earns is literally the title the Well stole, and it opens the §4 loop on the first sheet.
- **One engineering note.** The string `warden` is a kind key in two tables: `HVU.CFG.warden` and the game's slime archetype `warden` (19b line 44). The Roster, saves and the bestiary must namespace kind ids (`hvu:warden` / `hv:warden`) before the two meet in one store.

### 6.3 Other name fixes forced by the lineages
- **`pumpkinred` "THE HOLLOW KING" → THE LANTERN KING.** In this world "Hollow" means only the erasure and the Hollow Knight. A "HOLLOWED HOLLOW KING" callout would be unreadable. Its passive, KING OF FIRE, is untouched. The pumpkin's passive HOLLOW can stay, since it describes a hollow gourd, but **HUSK** would be cleaner.
- **`timewarden` keeps its display name, THE CHRONO TEMPLAR.** The brief calls it the "time warden", but on screen it never says Warden, so it does not collide.
- **`mirrorimage` stays hidden** and is never a Roster page: it is part of the Mirror Knight's page.

## 7. The hero: HALLOKIN, THE LAST WATCHMAN

### 7.1 Both are true; the name wins on screen
"Hallokin" exists only in the units guide (3 comments). "The watchman" exists only in the game's comment (`10-config.js` line 3) and in v1's fiction. Neither is shown to a player today, so nothing shipped has to change. Proposal: **the office is Watchman, and the name is Hallokin.** The title card, save slot and Chronicle read **HALLOKIN**. The role line reads "the last Watchman of the Vigil".

### 7.2 What the name means
- **The name is a signature.** Under the Draftsman's study sheet of the knight and the archer (v1: "two studies of the same figure") is one word in his hand: *Hallokin*. v1 already made a signature the one mark the Hollow respects (the Signed, teal). So **the hero is the first Signed figure**, and it is why he can walk into blank paper and keep a watched circle around him when no one else can.
- **The name is passed down.** The Watch has always had one Hallokin. Every Watchman who keeps the Vigil is drawn from that study and carries its name. **THE HOLLOW KNIGHT was Hallokin before you.** When he let go of the page, the Hollow took the one thing it respects, the signature, and he has had no name since. That is why the shipped boss is named only by what he became. The reveal ("his name was yours") lands at the Old Watch Barracks, from **THE REVENANT**. Its accent `#9aa0b0` is almost the Watch's `#9aa5b1`: the dead captain of the Hollow Knight's company, still in the Watch's grey.
- **Kin.** "Hallo-kin": the kin of the hall, the family of the Vigil. **Binding is countersigning.** When a spared body joins, it is signed into Hallokin's kin, and that is the fiction for v30's team-1 ownership: owned units never hit Hallokin and he never hits them (guide step 4), because they share a signature. "The *last* Watchman" is true at the start and false at the end. By the finale the Vigil is kept by a crowned Warden on every lamp, and **THE VIGIL IS KEPT · ENDLESS** becomes a plural sentence.

### 7.3 THE MIRROR KNIGHT: the capstone shaped like the hero
`mirrorknight`: MIRROR throws back 60% of hits while blocking, and MIRROR IMAGE sends out two images that fight for 8 s. That is the hero's own motif, one figure drawn twice. It is the Watch line's other finished figure, and it has **no region on purpose: it lives on the back of the page.** v1's dog-eared corners flip the page to its reverse, drawn with the `invert` path. A reversed drawing is a mirror. Each back-of-page secret can hold a Mirror Knight duel, and the last one guards the Hollow Knight's lair. Raising your own knight to THE MIRROR KNIGHT is the player choosing to become Hallokin twice: the unit that stands for the hero in his own army.

### 7.4 Team ids (a contract note, not fiction)
v30's code puts Hallokin on **team 1** with owned units and enemies on team 2. v1 had the player on team 0, with −1 as "leave me alone". The guide's `spawnFrom(...,{team:0})` is stale. The relations table from v1 §4.2 must be keyed on team 1 for the hero's side, and a neutral arrival in a clash can no longer use team 1 or team 2 as scratch. Houses need their own ids (3 and up), with `hostile(a,b)` doing the rest.

## 8. Risks and data fixes this lens found

1. **The team tint fights faction reading.** `drawInfo` multiplies every team-2 body by [1.0,0.72,0.72] (red) and every team-1 body by [0.86,0.92,1.0] (blue). The Kindled are already red, so a hostile Watch squad turns pink and a crowd of six houses reads as two. Houses on team ids 3 and up get no tint at all. **Recommendation:** drop the team multiply and keep accent colours pure. Show relation to the player with v1's thin underline (hostile red, ally ink), which also keeps colour crisp and saturated as the user demands.
2. **A capstone is mostly its parent, bigger.** 23 kinds borrow bodies, and three share `templar`: templar, warden at scale 1.22, timewarden at 1.25. Three share `priest`. **The Sentinel and the Chrono Templar are near-identical silhouettes.** The fiction supports "finished figure = the same figure, bolder and bigger", but the legibility constraint does not tolerate twins. **Recommendation:** each capstone gets a distinct tint *family* (Sentinel steel-blue, Chrono Templar gold), its crown glyph, and one signature ink FX of its own (a clock ring on REWIND, a mirror flash on MIRROR). No new sprites.
3. **The unlock numbers run backwards on three lines:** blackknight u7 under beamknight u9; werewolf u5 under hellhound u6; ghostfire u5 equal to eyeball u5. Region tier bands assume `unlock` rises along a line, so the children should be raised to at least their parent's value.
4. **FAMILY_MINIONS is keyed by group, not lineage** (§1). A conjuring Inquisitor raises bones. Key it by lineage root.
5. **Content load.** 87 Roster pages, 17 capstones, 9 lords and 10 champions. **Cut order:**
   1. fold the Kiln into Cinder Gate, so the Magma Colossus becomes the Scorch's second lord on one sheet (11 sheets);
   2. make the Lantern and Eye lines Rows mobs with no lord, so the Knife becomes the Rows' duel;
   3. turn the Three Kings into a single king fight;
   4. make champions without a region (Demon Brute, Werebear) bounty-board elites instead of duels.

   **Never cut:** Kindled as a house, the live lord evolutions, "capstones are grown, never bound", and the crown / Warden post.
6. **Power ceiling vs. the v1 caps.** A player army of crowned, ASCENDED capstones is a lot of units that each spawn summons: MASS GRAVE raises six, DEATH WINTER four, and MIRROR IMAGE, OUTBREAK and ROYAL SLAM add more. That collides with v1 §7.1's awake cap of 24. Posting crowned units on lamps (§4.2 rule 7) is also the pressure valve: the strongest bodies are usually *away* holding land.

1. **Core finding:** v30's EVOLVE graph is the world's family tree. Its 87 kinds fall into 21 lineages plus 3 loners, 17 tier-5 capstones and 15 cross-group "defection" edges. The world should be organised by lineage (root → capstone), not by v9 accent colour, and the capstones are the region lords.
2. **Rec 1 — six houses plus the Wild and the Dusk.**
   - The Watch gains THE SENTINEL (`warden`), THE MIRROR KNIGHT and the headhunter.
   - The Gilt Order gains THE CHRONO TEMPLAR, THE INQUISITOR and THE SPELLBLADE.
   - The Underdrawn get THE GRAVELORD and THE REVENANT.
   - The Redrafters get THE LICH and take the necromancer.
   - The Warband gets THE WARCHIEF, with iron-orc deserters and the rider.
   - New: **THE KINDLED**, the demons. They are colour taken instead of kept, and the third false cure (Burn) beside the Varnish and the Trace.
   - The shadow line becomes **the Dusk**, herded by the Hollow. Eyeball, ghostfire and pumpkins become the Lampless (snuffed lamps' eyes, flames and lanterns).
3. **Defection edges are the story:** axeman→ironorc, lancer→pike→rider, knife→verdagger, blade→spellblade and knight→templar are both the world's migrations and choices the player makes when evolving an owned unit.
4. **Rec 2 — capstones are grown, never bound.**
   - A capstone edge opens only once its Roster page is BESTED ("you cannot draw what you have never seen finished").
   - Every boss lord evolves live mid-fight through `HVU.evolve` (greatskel→gravelord, demonlord→abysslord and so on), so the player sees the rite before earning it.
5. After a lord falls: execute for its Law, or spare it to tend its lamp. Your first unit to evolve into that kind is crowned (`label` plus the crown glyph the demo already draws). You then post it as that lamp's WARDEN or march with it: land or army, never both.
6. **Rec 3 — 10 regions on 12 sheets, each a lineage home with a capstone lord:** Hatchwood (Storm Wing), Vellum Abbey (Chrono Templar), Tracing Tower (Lich), Brushlands ×2 (Warchief), Margin (Void Reaper; the Hollow Knight on the back of the page), Ashridge (the Warden), Palimpsest (Gravelord), Spill (the Three Kings), Rows (the Lantern King).
7. **The Scorch** (Cinder Gate: Abyss Lord; the Kiln: Magma Colossus) is drawn in *too much* colour, burning down to hard-edged dark char holes that are never white, which fits the colour rule. The Cinder Watch black knights are the Watch's secret shame.
8. **The Warden:** keep the key `warden`, rename the unit THE SENTINEL but move it into the Watch (watch → captain → sentinel), and make "Warden" a post held by crowned units on Great Lamps. The slime boss keeps THE WARDEN; your first crown is WARDEN OF ASHRIDGE.
9. **The hero is HALLOKIN, THE LAST WATCHMAN:** the name is the Draftsman's signature, passed down. The Hollow Knight was Hallokin before you and lost the name. Binding is countersigning ("kin"). Also rename pumpkinred's "THE HOLLOW KING" to THE LANTERN KING.
10. **Data fixes:** drop the team red/blue tint (it breaks faction reading), give the Sentinel and the Chrono Templar (both on `templar`) distinct tint and FX, make `unlock` rise along lines (blackknight, werewolf), key FAMILY_MINIONS by lineage, and namespace the two `warden` kind ids.
